## Relevant Topics

Keep papers whose CORE CONTRIBUTION is Mixture-of-Experts training, or the large-scale training systems and efficiency work that MoE training directly sits on. This is a narrow, specialist feed: precision matters far more than recall.

Broad frontier movement, model or product launches, and anything whose main contribution is downstream application belong in the hotspot digest, not here. When a paper merely uses MoE or distributed training as an off-the-shelf component, filter it.

1. MoE Training (primary)
- Keep: expert routing and gating mechanisms; load balancing and auxiliary losses; expert granularity, fine-grained and shared experts; MoE training stability (loss spikes, router collapse, z-loss and related fixes); expert and hybrid parallelism for MoE; all-to-all dispatch/combine and the communication schedules MoE needs; MoE kernels and grouped-GEMM style implementations; expert capacity, token dropping, and upcycling dense checkpoints into MoE.
- Filter: papers that train a MoE model to show a downstream result without a new routing, balancing, stability, parallelism, or kernel contribution; MoE used only as a baseline; "mixture of experts" in the classical ensemble or recommender sense.

2. Large-Scale Training Systems and Efficiency (adjacent)
- Keep: distributed training algorithms (data, tensor, pipeline, sequence, and expert parallelism, FSDP and ZeRO-style sharding); optimizers and preconditioners for large-scale pretraining; communication algorithms, overlap, and collective design; training-stability and throughput/MFU work that materially changes what a large training run costs or whether it converges; scaling-law work that directly informs how such runs are configured.
- Filter: inference-only serving, deployment, or latency work; quantization, pruning, and compression aimed at inference; routine infrastructure tuning without a new algorithmic or systems idea.

Leave these to the hotspot digest unless the core contribution clearly falls in one of the two topics above:
- major model or product releases
- agent, tooling, and RAG launches
- benchmark, leaderboard, or evaluation-only papers
- representation-learning theory, interpretability, and feature-formation analysis
- memory mechanisms and agent memory systems
- world models, exploration, and reinforcement learning
- alignment and post-training (RLHF, DPO, GRPO, RFT, instruction tuning)
- downstream applications in medical imaging, segmentation, 3D vision, video understanding, information retrieval, summarization, recommendation, machine translation, speech recognition, time series, knowledge graphs, and similar domains
