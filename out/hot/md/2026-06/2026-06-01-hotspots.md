  - arXiv 2605.31289 | Representation Learning Theory and Structure
  - Broadly important today: Introduces the terminal representation, a lower-dimensional reward-weighted RL representation that recovers DR-like structure without eigendecomposition.

  - arXiv 2605.31518 | Representation Learning Theory and Structure
